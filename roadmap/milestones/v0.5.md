# v0.5: model and algorithm coverage for TP, CP and their composition

Build on [v0.4](v0.4.md), initially with FSDP2, ordinary Ulysses CP, and a Wan model. This is a
proposed first integration choice, confirmed by the P06 probe. Additional transports and model
layouts enter stable support only after their own evidence. The target boundary is described in
[architecture direction](../design/architecture.md); test selection follows the harness delivered
by [v0.3](v0.3.md).

The next execution order is:

   expanding concrete CP admission. Its actual-parent and cumulative gates pass.

For support closure, distinguish three evidence layers: shared mechanism tests, each concrete
algorithm/model path, and critical interactions such as replay, roles, evaluation, overlap and
resume. A changed loss does not automatically require all backends; a changed shared backward
or preparation owner does require the affected backend mechanisms. The catalog and semantic
selection rules choose those cases; neither inheritance nor a giant Cartesian product is a proof.

Complete P11e/P11g and applicable algorithm/model intersections before beta. The subsequent

findings. Do not call v0.5 complete while a required cell is merely untested or silently deferred.
