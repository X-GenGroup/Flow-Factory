# v0.5: model and algorithm coverage for TP, CP and their composition

Build on [v0.4](v0.4.md), initially with FSDP2, ordinary Ulysses CP, and a Wan model. This is a
proposed first integration choice, confirmed by the P06 probe. Additional transports and model
layouts enter stable support only after their own evidence. The target boundary is described in
[architecture direction](../design/architecture.md); test selection follows the harness delivered
by [v0.3](v0.3.md).

- **Status:** in_progress
- **Depends on:** [P08](v0.4.md#p08), with the P05/P06 topology and preparation results
- **Coordination:** [PR #290](https://github.com/X-GenGroup/Flow-Factory/pull/290), in native stack #291 after #289

**Review checkpoint (2026-11-05).** Implementation and exact-source qualification are delivered

**Outcome.** Wan training runs correctly with CP and TP=1, using a declared supported configuration.

**Scope.** Connect Diffusers' verified CP plan/attention path to FF's mesh and lifecycle. Preserve
global positions, conditions, logical sample/RNG identity and gradient normalization. Initially
restore full denoiser output at the adapter boundary to reuse scheduler and trajectory behavior.

**Acceptance.**

- Compare forward output, elementwise gradients and optimizer updates with CP disabled at fixed
  logical sample/update geometry. Cover the selected precision and activation recomputation.
- Verify attention context, conditioning, token/position order and loss scaling; finite gradients
  alone are insufficient.
- Reject unsupported masks/layouts and dependency combinations early, and document the precise
  experimental capability unlocked by this alpha.

**Outcome.** CP preserves online/offline, multi-role, feedback and checkpoint behavior for the

**Scope.** Extend runtime identity and checkpoint metadata for CP; use the state mechanisms from

  guarantees. An unsupported exact-resume layout fails before state mutation.

<a id="p11-review-checkpoint"></a>
### Review checkpoint: 2026-11-05

**Implemented locally.** Explicit concrete-trainer CP admission for SFT/GRPO/DMD2;
full-output loss normalization at the existing backward owner; preparation of every physical
full/LoRA variant; TP x CP reward work sharing; CP runtime identity and drained-boundary state
continuation with EMA/reference/named snapshots. Evaluation, arbitrary in-flight recovery and
unqualified trainers remain gated. See [context parallelism](../../guidance/context_parallel.md).

**Integration fixes.** FSDP2 exact checkpoint zero is rejected before Torch's serializer can
initialize an empty optimizer. Shared snapshot writes settle pending gathers and discard retained
full-parameter views, including restoration around a pending policy graph. Late variants retain
FSDP2's FP32 original dtype before TP creates DTensor metadata. These fixes remain at shared
checkpoint/preparation/precision owners rather than in individual algorithms.

**Numerical findings.** The observed guided-GRPO failures were cross-CP trajectory gradient/moment
differences, not rollout/replay failures. Actual velocity, transition mean and log probability
agree exactly in the tested native path, including fixed-topology restore. Native Ulysses uses
partition exchange and ordered gather; no global NCCL Ring/Simple policy was introduced.

DMD2's fake/reference score cancellation amplifies small BF16 differences. In the retained LoRA
cases, both generator updates exceed the original cross-trajectory `4/128` delta cap. Bidirectional
common-state controls pass the unchanged gradient gates at both actual histories. Independent
clipping/FP64 Adam histories predict the observed update differences; actual-to-same-state
predictions pass the original local update cap and derived elementwise bounds. Original failed
trajectory limits remain explicit diagnostics. This narrow DMD2 criterion proves local CP and
optimizer semantics on the tested histories; it does not promise close learned adapters,
closed-loop trajectories or convergence. SFT and GRPO do not inherit that update exception.

**Evidence boundary.** Current P11 results are local development evidence, not an accepted
immutable R4 plan or a release qualification. Ten online full/LoRA x TP1/TP2 cases pass,
including guided GRPO, bidirectional DMD2 controls and fresh-process continuation; 294 focused
CPU checks pass. Four focused snapshot cases cover full/LoRA, TP,
recomputation, pending prefetch/graphs, exception restoration and rejection of reentrant writes.
A two-node CP-boundary diagnostic passes guided GRPO replay/resume and full DMD2 continuation.
The local review report retains complete commands, failures, tensor measurements and independent
numerical reviews under the ignored investigation directory.

**Remaining before delivery.** Freeze and review the complete source; select and run the exact-source
R4 plan across all affected shared owners/backends; repeat the required physical TP/CP/DP
boundaries on that source; archive durable evidence; commit coherent fixes and P11 changes;
open the stacked PR on #292 and monitor its checks. No P11 PR, merge or release tag exists yet.
The user reviewed this checkpoint and authorized resuming with P11a. The findings above remain
historical development evidence; the child PRs require their own final-source qualification.

## P12: expand model coverage and publish measured capabilities

- **Status:** planned
- **Depends on:** [P10](#p10); P11 and the coverage disposition are required for the beta tag
- **Coordination:** unclaimed
- **Release boundary:** `v0.5.0-beta.1`, after P11 and P12

**Outcome.** Maximize applicable TP, CP and TP+CP coverage across the existing model and trainer
registries, with SP where the model supports it. A second model is an early design check, not
the completion criterion.

**Scope.** Start from the 2026-11-05 registry (19 model identifiers, 14 trainers). Derive the
coverage inventory from existing capabilities and validation cases; do not create a competing
support registry. Each cell identifies the real checkpoint/layout, task, algorithm, parallel mode,
degree, full/LoRA, precision, dependency tuple and evidence. Track missing implementation,
unverified behavior, numerical defects and capacity separately. A semantically inapplicable cell
needs a concrete reason; a missing plan or test is unfinished work, not inapplicability.

**Model delivery batches.** Each independent model structure is a separate PR; shared family
entries still require their distinct conditioning/layout cases.

| Batch | Models | Work |
|---|---|---|
| First structure | Wan T2V/I2V | Added image KV, per-token timesteps, Wan2.2 component schedules |
| Dual stream | Flux1/Kontext, Qwen Image/Edit | Native CP plans, TP boundaries, reference images, positions and output cropping |
| Additional dense layouts | SD3.5, Z-Image | CP plans, real head-count constraints and conditional Ring qualification |
| Audio/video | LTX2 T2AV/I2AV | Distinct stream lengths, cross-modal attention, outputs and gradient ownership |
| Prefix/packed | Qwen-Image-2.1, Bagel, SenseNova | Real-layout TP defects, KV cache, block masks, packed replay and ordered references |
| Packed projections | Flux2/Klein/Flux3 | CP independently of TP; reversible packed TP and checkpoint mapping before TP admission |
| Large multi-component | MiniMax H3 T2VA/FL2VA/Ref2VA | Loading/materialization peaks before TP; component and audio/video semantics |

Ordinary Ulysses remains the first path. Evaluate ordinary Ring when actual head divisibility
prevents a useful composition, with backward and fixed rollout/replay combination order proved
before admission. Do not enable inference-only Anything attention for training. Uneven tokens
need verified masking, padding and output/loss cropping. Relax batch-size and topology guards
only with evidence, including logical-DP=1 when supported by the backend reduction contract.

**Acceptance.**

- Every published model/algorithm/mode cell has a short real training-path check, including
  nontrivial updates and role/state behavior; a declared plan alone does not qualify training.
- Deep numerical/interaction evidence covers full/LoRA, declared SP modes, recomputation,
  masks/conditioning, eval, overlap and continuation wherever affected. Real-weight canaries
  are selected per structural risk and never replaced by a differently shaped tiny model.
- The known Qwen-Image-2.1 TP discrepancy and packed/loading restrictions retain explicit
  reproductions and disposition. User decisions, not silent exclusions, resolve feasible
  unfinished release goals.
- Coupled-policy rollout/replay keeps matching inputs, precision, graph and reduction order.
  The demonstrated DMD2 generator numerical exception does not propagate to other algorithms.

## Delivery sequence and release gates

The user authorized this sequence on 2026-11-05. P11a-P11d form the initial stack on #292.
Evaluation, overlap geometry and algorithm/model extensions then form short dependency stacks;
each feature PR has its own evidence and every pushed head must have its checks monitored and
repaired before dependent delivery continues. Existing P09/P10 claims remain historical to their
own commits. No release tag or merge is implied by opening these PRs.

Complete P11e/P11g and applicable algorithm/model intersections before beta. P13 performs
final cross-node TP/CP/DP placements, dependency/compatibility checks and measured whole-step
performance (including precision islands, snapshot gathers and loading peaks). P14 closes RC
findings. Do not call v0.5 complete while a required cell is merely untested or silently deferred.
Arbitrary in-flight recovery, topology-changing optimizer continuation and EP remain outside
this delivery; record a user decision for any other reduction of the accepted scope.
