## Separate asynchronous decision

The user's 2026-11-06 acceptance of reward-completion and floating accumulation-order variation
is distinct from these CP numerical exceptions. It permits tolerance-based comparison of the same
sample/weight/parameter-state membership; it does not permit divergent collective order. P11g's
late-future-failure WORLD-versus-CP race is a confirmed correctness defect with a narrow CPU/Gloo
tested ordering correction. Its remaining native/overlap acceptance is tracked in
[P11g](../milestones/v0.5.md#p11g), and CP overlap remains closed.
Moving samples across optimizer-step boundaries is a scheduling-semantic change, not merely
floating accumulation order. Validate exactly-once membership, weights, accumulation and role
cadence, strict same-policy replay and valid ready-mode continuation without demanding identical
future completion order or trajectories.
