## Separate asynchronous decision

The user's 2026-11-06 acceptance of reward-completion and floating accumulation-order variation
is distinct from these CP numerical exceptions. It permits tolerance-based comparison of the same
sample/weight/parameter-state membership; it does not permit divergent collective order. P11g's
late-future-failure WORLD-versus-CP race is a confirmed correctness defect with a narrow CPU/Gloo
tested ordering correction. The P11g candidate now includes that correction and native A–C
scenarios. Development probes pass; final exact-source qualification is required by
[P11g](../milestones/v0.5.md#p11g) and recorded in its published PR evidence. Accepted asynchronous
variation does not waive the correction.
Moving samples across optimizer-step boundaries is a scheduling-semantic change, not merely
floating accumulation order. Validate exactly-once membership, weights, accumulation and role
cadence, strict same-policy replay and valid ready-mode continuation without demanding identical
future completion order or trajectories.

The final A attempt at `fcbdf7c436335ad9c7204d23734c3f21ad110b78` failed its future-state
bitwise assertion after an exact checkpoint load. Every retained rank preserved ordered work
identity; the first differing gradient element had absolute error `1.862645149230957e-09`,
and final Adam moment errors were `1.6763834764788044e-10` and `1.4176155163484784e-18`.
The original assertion and native result remain failed. The reviewed correction at `064f8b8d`
keeps loaded state, ordered membership, RNG, progress and runtime identity exact, reuses the
existing BF16 gradient/update bounds without an SFT LoRA exception, and checks independent
Adam/EMA equations. Both schedules bind final snapshots to their own observed optimizer state.
CPU rechecks of retained A/D/E records are diagnostic only; the corrected immutable source
requires fresh selected native qualification. No previous receipt is relabeled as that pass.
